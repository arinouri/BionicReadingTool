Why these tools

Common requests.
Simple software.

People regularly ask for text-to-speech with highlighting, distraction-free reading, basic writing insights, and citation formatting without subscriptions or premium prompts. ReadEasier keeps those functions small, local, and available.

  • Text stays in this browser.
  • Results are never blurred or locked.
  • No AI-generated rewrite replaces your wording.
  • Every control works without signing in.
